📸 Fun Booth

AI photo booth with themes, prints and AirDrop

🖥️

Guest screen

iPad facing the guests: theme carousel, live preview, countdown and the finished photo.

 🎛️

Operator

iPad or laptop: themes, camera, print and send. Can also be the camera.

 📷

Camera

Use an iPhone, iPad or laptop as the booth camera.

Just one iPad? Open the guest screen and choose “Camera + guest screen”. The ⚙︎ corner button opens a PIN-protected host menu for printing and sending.
Tip: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app. Every device needs the booth PIN.
