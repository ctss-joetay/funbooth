Pick a booth mode

📱

One iPad

One iPad does everything: camera, guest screen, printing and sending.

  1. iPadOpen Guest screen → choose One iPad. Print and send from the ⚙︎ host menu (PIN).
💻

iPad + laptop + camera

Guests see themselves on the iPad; the operator works from a laptop; a phone takes the photos (or the laptop's built-in or USB camera).

  1. iPadOpen Guest screen → choose iPad + laptop + camera
  2. LaptopOpen Operator to print and send
  3. PhoneOpen Camera → Start camera
📱📱

Two iPads

Guests see themselves on one iPad; the operator's iPad takes the photos, prints and sends.

  1. Guest iPadOpen Guest screen → choose Two iPads
  2. Operator iPadOpen Operator and point its back camera at the guests
🖥️ Guest screen 🎛️ Operator 📷 Camera

Every device needs the booth PIN. You can switch modes any time in Operator → Settings or the guest screen's host menu.
Tip: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.
